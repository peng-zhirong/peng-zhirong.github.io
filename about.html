Curiosity in the lab.
Clarity beyond it.

My path into device physics began with electrical engineering and hands-on sensing research. Today, I study the atomic details that matter when transistors become extremely narrow.

I’m pursuing my Ph.D. at HKUST with Prof. Mansun Chan. My background brings together materials, fabrication, electrical testing and quantum transport, helping me connect a physical mechanism with an engineering question.

Beyond research, I have worked in visual design, public speaking, teaching and student mentorship. Explaining an idea clearly is an important part of how I work.
